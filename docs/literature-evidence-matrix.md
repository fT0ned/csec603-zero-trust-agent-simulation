# Literature evidence matrix and design traceability

Authors' analytical artifact; primary-source audit date: **23 October 2026**. Reference numbers refer to the original Report 1 bibliography, not to a new bibliography. This targeted verification is not a systematic review or a claim of exhaustive retrieval. It preserves Report 1's 16 evidence-table entries and adds explicit design relevance and verification status. The original RQ wording must be retained in the manuscript; the RQ labels below identify its three identity, delegation and attribution themes.

The source hierarchy is the official Report 2 requirements and professor feedback, followed by the central Phase 2 document. This matrix does not modify the frozen protocol or select experimental parameters from observed security outcomes. Standards justify architecture; published measurements provide context; neither supplies this study's results. Numbers below belong to the cited authors and must never be combined with testbed observations into one effect estimate.

## Verification method and interpretation

The audit opened the original DOI, standards URL, proceedings page or arXiv record. Where a publisher blocked retrieval, an official conference program was used only to verify title/authors/venue, not numerical claims. No secondary aggregator was used as substantive evidence. See the [32-reference verification register](reference-verification.md) for access limits and corrections. “Original rating” reproduces Report 1's qualitative appraisal; it is not a new validated score. “Unverified claim” means this audit could not independently inspect supporting primary text, not that the source or claim is false.

The synthesis supports testing three distinct questions: whether a finite bearer lifetime bounds replay, whether trusted action/workflow scope contains authority beyond authentication, and whether protected provenance distinguishes observed coercion from replay. It does not establish in advance that any particular condition improves outcomes. Fixed author-selected payloads, a single deployed model alias, and one host remain limits even when all runs complete. Final manuscript RQ answers require the independently sealed full-study results and their uncertainty.

## Manuscript integration checklist

1. Preserve Report 1 reference numbering while editing; renumber only through the manuscript bibliography tool and check every in-text citation afterward.
2. Pin [17], [18] and [27] to the exact reviewed arXiv version. A revised preprint is not automatically peer-reviewed because its template contains a journal name.
3. For each unverified numerical claim, obtain the primary paper through institutional access or an author-hosted accepted manuscript and record page/table, denominator, metric definition and version. Until then omit its numerical claim from authoritative synthesis or explicitly identify it as unverified carry-forward text.
4. Cite this matrix and interpret its limits in Related Work. Keep the new testbed observations in their own Results section; literature figures cannot fill missing measurements.
5. Confirm the final majority of sources are peer-reviewed or authoritative guidance, preserve preprint labels, and run the bidirectional citation/reference check. This audit does not certify the entire bibliography as verified.
